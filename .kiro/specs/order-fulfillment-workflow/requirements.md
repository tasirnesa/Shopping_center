# Requirements Document

## Introduction

The Order Fulfillment Workflow is a multi-stage business process for a pharmacy distributor operating on a NestJS (backend) and React Native/Expo (mobile) platform. It covers the complete lifecycle of a customer order, from initial sales representative contact through invoice verification, warehouse picking and packing, delivery, and final completion. The workflow enforces strict role-based access control across five actor roles — Sales Representative, Invoice Maker, Store Man, Driver, and Manager — and provides real-time status visibility via dashboards and mobile applications.

The full order status progression is:
**Draft → Submitted → Waiting for Invoice → Invoice Approved → Waiting for Warehouse → Picking → Packed → Ready for Delivery → Out for Delivery → Delivered → Completed**

Side-path statuses: **Rejected**, **Cancelled**, **Returned**

---

## Glossary

- **Sales_Order**: A document created by a Sales Representative capturing customer details, product lines, and supporting document attachments, subject to a defined status workflow.
- **Sales_Order_Line**: A single product entry within a Sales Order, containing product reference, quantity, unit price, discount, and computed total.
- **Invoice**: A tax invoice generated by the Invoice Maker from an approved Sales Order, containing invoice number, tax amount, VAT, and grand total.
- **Invoice_Line**: A single product line within an Invoice mirroring the Sales Order Line with finalized pricing.
- **Picking_List**: A warehouse document generated for the Store Man listing all products and quantities to be physically picked from stock for an order.
- **Delivery_Note**: A document generated from a packed order containing customer address, phone, product list, and invoice copy reference, used by the Driver.
- **Delivery**: A record tracking a Driver's delivery assignment for a specific order, including status updates and delivery confirmation.
- **Sales_Representative**: A system user with the SALES_REP role who creates and manages Sales Orders and customer records.
- **Invoice_Maker**: A system user with the INVOICE_MAKER role who verifies Sales Orders and generates Tax Invoices.
- **Store_Man**: A system user with the STORE_MAN role who manages warehouse picking, packing, and stock updates.
- **Driver**: A system user with the DRIVER role who executes deliveries and confirms receipt.
- **Manager**: A system user with the MANAGER role who has read-only access to all modules and the full dashboard.
- **TIN**: Tax Identification Number — a mandatory customer identifier for invoice generation.
- **Trade_License**: A regulatory document uploaded as an attachment to a Sales Order to verify the customer's trading authorization.
- **Payment_Receipt**: A financial document uploaded as an attachment to a Sales Order confirming customer payment.
- **Stock_Balance**: The current on-hand quantity of a product at a specific branch.
- **Stock_Transaction**: A record of every inventory movement (deduction or addition) tied to a reference order or adjustment.
- **Organization**: The top-level tenant entity to which all data belongs, as defined by the existing system.
- **Branch**: A physical location within an Organization, as defined by the existing system.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Sales Order Creation

**User Story:** As a Sales Representative, I want to create a Sales Order with customer details, product lines, and required document attachments, so that I can capture a customer's purchase intent and submit it for invoice processing.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Sales_Order SHALL contain the following mandatory fields: customer name, TIN number, delivery address, at least one Sales_Order_Line, a Trade_License attachment, and a Payment_Receipt attachment.
2. WHEN a Sales Representative creates a Sales_Order, THE Sales_Order SHALL be assigned the status **Draft** upon initial creation.
3. THE Sales_Order_Line SHALL contain the following fields: product reference, quantity (positive integer greater than zero), unit price, discount (non-negative value), and a computed total equal to `(unit_price × quantity) - discount`.
4. WHEN a Sales Representative submits a Sales_Order in **Draft** status, THE Sales_Order SHALL transition to **Submitted** status.
5. IF a Sales Representative attempts to submit a Sales_Order that is missing a mandatory field, THEN THE System SHALL return a validation error listing each missing field.
6. IF a Sales Representative attempts to submit a Sales_Order with a Sales_Order_Line quantity less than or equal to zero, THEN THE System SHALL return a validation error for that line.
7. THE Sales_Representative SHALL create, read, and update Sales Orders that belong to their own user account only. THE Sales_Representative SHALL NOT access Sales Orders created by other users.
8. WHEN a Sales Order attachment is uploaded, THE System SHALL accept file types PDF, JPG, JPEG, and PNG with a maximum individual file size of 10 MB.
9. THE Sales_Order SHALL support the following attachment types: Trade_License (required), Payment_Receipt (required), Purchase Order (optional), and Other Documents (optional, multiple allowed).
10. WHEN a Sales Representative views their Sales Orders list, THE System SHALL return all Sales Orders associated with the Sales Representative's user account sorted by creation date descending.

### Requirement 2: Invoice Verification and Generation

**User Story:** As an Invoice Maker, I want to review submitted Sales Orders, verify all required documents and product information, and generate a Tax Invoice, so that orders are financially validated before being released to the warehouse.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a Sales_Order is in **Submitted** status, THE Invoice_Maker SHALL be able to view the Sales Order details including all attached documents, product lines, customer TIN, and delivery address.
2. WHEN the Invoice_Maker approves a Sales_Order, THE System SHALL generate an Invoice with a unique sequential Invoice Number scoped to the Organization, a tax amount computed as `subtotal × tax_rate`, VAT, and a grand total equal to `subtotal + tax_amount`.
3. WHEN the Invoice_Maker approves a Sales_Order, THE Sales_Order SHALL transition from **Submitted** to **Waiting for Invoice** to **Invoice Approved** and THE System SHALL notify the Store_Man role.
4. WHEN an Invoice is generated, THE Invoice SHALL contain: Invoice Number, Invoice Date, Customer Name, TIN Number, Delivery Address, one Invoice_Line per Sales_Order_Line, tax amount, VAT percentage, and grand total.
5. WHEN the Invoice_Maker rejects a Sales_Order, THE Invoice_Maker SHALL provide a rejection reason with a minimum length of 10 characters, and THE Sales_Order SHALL transition to **Rejected** status.
6. IF the Invoice_Maker attempts to approve a Sales_Order with a missing Trade_License attachment, THEN THE System SHALL block the approval and return an error indicating the missing document.
7. IF the Invoice_Maker attempts to approve a Sales_Order with a missing Payment_Receipt attachment, THEN THE System SHALL block the approval and return an error indicating the missing document.
8. THE Invoice_Maker SHALL NOT modify Sales_Order_Line quantities, unit prices, or product selections after the Sales Order has been submitted.
9. WHEN the Invoice_Maker views the pending orders list, THE System SHALL display only Sales Orders in **Submitted** status, sorted by submission date ascending (oldest first).
10. THE Invoice SHALL be immutable after generation. THE System SHALL reject any attempt to modify an Invoice after its creation.

### Requirement 3: Warehouse Picking and Packing

**User Story:** As a Store Man, I want to view approved invoices, pick and pack the corresponding products, update stock levels, and print a Delivery Note, so that orders are physically prepared and ready for dispatch.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a Sales_Order reaches **Invoice Approved** status, THE Store_Man SHALL be able to view the corresponding Picking_List containing product names, SKUs, quantities, and bin locations.
2. WHEN the Store_Man initiates picking on an approved order, THE Sales_Order SHALL transition from **Waiting for Warehouse** to **Picking** status.
3. WHEN the Store_Man confirms all items are picked for an order in **Picking** status, THE Sales_Order SHALL transition to **Packed** status and THE System SHALL deduct the corresponding quantities from Stock_Balance for each product in the order.
4. WHEN stock is deducted upon packing confirmation, THE System SHALL create a Stock_Transaction record for each product line with type **OUT**, quantity deducted, and a reference to the Sales_Order ID.
5. IF a product's Stock_Balance at the branch would fall below zero after deduction, THEN THE System SHALL block the packing confirmation and return an insufficient stock error listing each affected product and its current available quantity.
6. WHEN the Store_Man marks an order as **Packed**, THE Sales_Order SHALL transition to **Ready for Delivery** status and THE System SHALL make the Delivery_Note available for printing.
7. THE Delivery_Note SHALL contain: order reference number, customer name, delivery address, customer phone number, product list with quantities, and a reference to the Invoice Number.
8. WHEN the Store_Man prints a Picking_List or Delivery_Note, THE System SHALL generate a printable PDF document containing the relevant fields.
9. THE Store_Man SHALL NOT modify Invoice details, unit prices, or Sales_Order_Line quantities.
10. WHERE the mobile application is used, THE Store_Man SHALL be able to confirm product picking by scanning a product barcode to match it against the Picking_List line item.

---

### Requirement 4: Delivery Execution

**User Story:** As a Driver, I want to view my assigned deliveries, access customer address and contact details, and confirm delivery completion with an upload, so that orders are marked as delivered with proof of receipt.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a Sales_Order reaches **Ready for Delivery** status, THE System SHALL create a Delivery record assigned to the Driver role, containing the customer delivery address, customer phone number, and a reference to the Invoice.
2. WHEN a Driver picks up an order for delivery, THE Driver SHALL update the Delivery status to **Out for Delivery** and THE Sales_Order SHALL transition to **Out for Delivery** status.
3. WHEN a Driver confirms delivery of an order, THE Driver SHALL upload a delivery confirmation document or image, and THE Sales_Order SHALL transition to **Delivered** status.
4. WHEN a Sales_Order reaches **Delivered** status, THE System SHALL automatically transition the Sales_Order to **Completed** status upon successful delivery confirmation upload.
5. THE Delivery record SHALL contain: Delivery ID, Sales Order reference, Invoice reference, customer name, delivery address, customer phone number, assigned driver user ID, status, and delivery confirmation attachment path.
6. WHERE the mobile application is used, THE Driver SHALL be able to view a delivery list filtered to their assigned deliveries only.
7. WHERE the mobile application is used, THE Driver SHALL be able to access navigation directions from the delivery address stored in the Delivery record.
8. IF a Driver attempts to mark an order as delivered without uploading a delivery confirmation, THEN THE System SHALL return a validation error requiring the confirmation upload.
9. THE Driver SHALL NOT view, modify, or access Sales Orders, Invoices, or Picking Lists. THE Driver SHALL access only Delivery records assigned to their user account.

---

### Requirement 5: Order Cancellation and Returns

**User Story:** As a Sales Representative or Manager, I want to cancel or initiate a return on an order at appropriate stages in the workflow, so that exceptions are handled without corrupting inventory or financial records.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a Sales Representative cancels a Sales_Order in **Draft** or **Submitted** status, THE Sales_Order SHALL transition to **Cancelled** status and no Invoice or stock change SHALL be created.
2. WHEN a Manager cancels a Sales_Order in any status before **Out for Delivery**, THE Sales_Order SHALL transition to **Cancelled** status and THE System SHALL record the cancellation reason with a minimum length of 10 characters.
3. IF a Sales_Order in **Picking**, **Packed**, or **Ready for Delivery** status is cancelled, THEN THE System SHALL reverse any Stock_Balance deductions that were applied, and THE System SHALL create a compensating Stock_Transaction record for each reversed line with type **IN**.
4. WHEN a return is initiated on a **Completed** or **Delivered** Sales_Order, THE Sales_Order SHALL transition to **Returned** status and THE System SHALL create a compensating Stock_Transaction record for each returned product line with type **IN**.
5. THE System SHALL preserve all original Sales_Order, Invoice, and Delivery records in a read-only state after cancellation or return. THE System SHALL NOT delete any historical records.

---

### Requirement 6: Role-Based Access Control

**User Story:** As a system administrator, I want each role to be restricted to only the actions and data relevant to their function, so that data integrity and operational security are maintained across the workflow.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Sales_Representative SHALL only access the following modules: Customer management (create, read, update), Sales Order management (create, read, update own orders), and document attachment upload.
2. THE Invoice_Maker SHALL only access the following modules: Sales Order review (read all submitted), Invoice generation (create), and Sales Order rejection (update status to Rejected). THE Invoice_Maker SHALL NOT modify inventory or stock records.
3. THE Store_Man SHALL only access the following modules: Approved Invoice view (read), Picking List management (read, update picking status), Delivery Note generation (read, print), and Stock Balance update (write on packing confirmation only). THE Store_Man SHALL NOT modify Invoice records.
4. THE Driver SHALL only access Delivery records assigned to their user account and SHALL NOT access Sales Orders, Invoices, Picking Lists, or Stock Balances.
5. THE Manager SHALL have read access to all modules including Sales Orders, Invoices, Picking Lists, Deliveries, Stock Balances, and Dashboard metrics. THE Manager SHALL NOT perform transactional writes such as creating Sales Orders or generating Invoices.
6. WHEN a user attempts to access a resource outside their permitted role, THE System SHALL return an HTTP 403 Forbidden response.
7. WHEN a user attempts to access a Sales_Order that does not belong to their user account and their role is Sales_Representative, THE System SHALL return an HTTP 403 Forbidden response.

---

### Requirement 7: Dashboard and Reporting

**User Story:** As a Manager or role-specific user, I want to see relevant workflow metrics on a dashboard, so that I can monitor operational status and identify bottlenecks in real time.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Dashboard SHALL display the following Sales section metrics: count of Sales Orders created today, count of Sales Orders in **Submitted** status (Pending Orders), and count of Sales Orders in **Waiting for Invoice** status.
2. THE Dashboard SHALL display the following Invoice section metrics: count of Sales Orders in **Submitted** status awaiting approval (Waiting Approval), and count of Invoices generated today.
3. THE Dashboard SHALL display the following Warehouse section metrics: count of Sales Orders in **Waiting for Warehouse** status (Waiting Picking), and count of Sales Orders in **Ready for Delivery** status.
4. THE Dashboard SHALL display the following Delivery section metrics: count of Deliveries in **Out for Delivery** status, and count of Deliveries with **Delivered** status updated today.
5. WHEN a user accesses the Dashboard, THE System SHALL return only metrics relevant to the requesting user's role. THE Sales_Representative SHALL see only Sales metrics. THE Invoice_Maker SHALL see only Invoice metrics. THE Store_Man SHALL see only Warehouse metrics. THE Driver SHALL see only Delivery metrics. THE Manager SHALL see all sections.
6. WHEN a Manager requests a Sales Orders report, THE System SHALL return all Sales Orders filterable by status, date range, Sales Representative, and branch.

---

### Requirement 8: Mobile Application Support

**User Story:** As a Sales Representative, Store Man, or Driver using a mobile device, I want to access the core functions of my role through a mobile application, so that I can perform my work tasks away from a desktop.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHERE the mobile application is used by a Sales_Representative, THE Mobile_App SHALL provide: a customer list with search, a Sales Order creation form including document upload for Trade_License and Payment_Receipt, and a submit action for Draft orders.
2. WHERE the mobile application is used by a Store_Man, THE Mobile_App SHALL provide: a Picking List view filtered to orders in **Waiting for Warehouse** status, a barcode scan function to confirm individual product picking, and a confirm-all action to transition the order to **Packed** status.
3. WHERE the mobile application is used by a Driver, THE Mobile_App SHALL provide: a delivery list filtered to the Driver's assigned deliveries, an address display with navigation link, and a delivery confirmation upload function.
4. WHEN the mobile application submits a document upload, THE System SHALL accept the upload via the same API endpoints used by the web application and apply the same file type and size validation rules.
5. WHEN a mobile user's authentication token expires, THE Mobile_App SHALL redirect the user to the login screen and SHALL NOT expose a previously authenticated session.

---

### Requirement 9: Audit Trail and Data Integrity

**User Story:** As a Manager, I want every status transition and significant action to be recorded with a timestamp and actor identity, so that the full history of an order can be reviewed for compliance and dispute resolution.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE System SHALL record a status change event for every Sales_Order status transition, capturing: previous status, new status, timestamp, and the user ID of the actor who performed the transition.
2. WHEN an Invoice is generated, THE System SHALL record the Invoice Maker's user ID, the timestamp of generation, and the associated Sales_Order ID.
3. WHEN a Stock_Transaction is created, THE System SHALL record the type (IN or OUT), quantity, product ID, branch ID, timestamp, and the reference Sales_Order ID or adjustment reason.
4. WHEN a Delivery confirmation is uploaded, THE System SHALL record the Driver's user ID, the timestamp of upload, and the file path of the confirmation document.
5. THE System SHALL preserve audit records indefinitely. THE System SHALL NOT delete status change events, Invoice generation records, Stock_Transaction records, or Delivery confirmation records.
